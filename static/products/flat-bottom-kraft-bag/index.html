Product description

The square base means this bag stands up on its own, so customers can fill it at a refill station or you can pack it with both hands. The top edge is serrated, and it folds over neatly for a seal sticker or a peg.

It measures 180 mm wide with a 110 mm gusset and 300 mm height, which holds around 2 kg of fruit and vegetables, 1 kg of oats or pasta, or a loaf with room left over. Delis use it for cheese and cured meats wrapped in greaseproof paper first.

The kraft is unbleached and suitable for direct contact with dry food. Bags are recyclable at the kerbside and will also break down in a home compost heap.
