Product description

A single round sticker is the neatest way to close tissue paper inside a box, fold over the top of a paper bag or seal a pillow box. These are cut from unbleached kraft paper with a permanent adhesive, supplied on a roll that fits a small dispenser or sits beside the packing bench.

At 38 mm across they are large enough for a stamped logo, a handwritten name or a best-before date. Bakeries use them to date window bags; makers use them as a plain finishing touch.

The paper face and adhesive are suitable for paper recycling, so a bag or box sealed with one can go in the kerbside bin without the sticker being peeled off.
