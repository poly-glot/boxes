Product description

Photographers, illustrators, printers and solicitors' offices use these to post anything that cannot be bent. A rigid greyboard panel is bonded to the back of a manila envelope, so the contents stay flat even when the envelope is handled roughly.

The C4 size takes unfolded A4 sheets, A4 prints and certificates with a little room to spare. The peel-and-seal flap closes securely and opens cleanly with a tear strip.

Both the envelope and the board are paper, so the whole thing goes into kerbside paper recycling without being taken apart.
