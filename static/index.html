Less plastic, less air, fewer vans

Most of what we sell is paper and board, much of it with recycled content, and we label clearly how each item can be recycled. Where plastic is still the right tool, we stock versions made with recycled material. We also help you choose box sizes that waste less space, and we take back pallets and clean cardboard from local trade customers.
