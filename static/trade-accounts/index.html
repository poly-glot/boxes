Trade accounts

Buy on 30-day terms, with one person who knows your business and your usual order.

Who can apply

Trade accounts are for UK businesses, charities, schools and public bodies that buy packaging regularly. You do not need to spend a minimum amount, but we usually open accounts for businesses that expect to order at least once a month. Sole traders are welcome.

How 30-day terms work

Approved accounts can pay by invoice 30 days from the end of the month in which goods are dispatched. Terms are subject to approval: we carry out a credit check when you apply, and we may start with a lower credit limit and review it after three months. You can still pay by card at any time.

Your account manager

Every trade account has a named account manager in our customer team. They will learn what you usually order, remind you when you are running low if you would like them to, and help with sizing, samples and custom printing. You will have their direct line and email.

Benefits

  • 30-day payment terms, subject to approval
  • A named account manager with a direct line
  • Bulk discounts applied to every order, as shown on product pages
  • Monthly statements and invoices sent to your accounts team
  • Purchase order references on every invoice
  • Repeat orders by phone or email in a single message
  • Free take-back of clean cardboard and pallets within 25 miles of Northampton
  • Early notice of price changes and new ranges

What happens next

  1. Send the application form below.
  2. We run a credit check and reply within 2 working days.
  3. Your account manager calls to introduce themselves and set up your account.
  4. You place your first order on account.
